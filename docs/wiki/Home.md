# Buddhi Align Wiki

Welcome. I am [Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com/), and I created [Buddhi Align](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/) in the hope that attentive reflection may help us meet one another with greater patience, courage, and care. The app is currently deployed at [https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/). This wiki is a shared doorway into the work: a place to learn, begin gently, and help improve it.

**Author:** [Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com/)

Buddhi Align supports a simple daily rhythm: form an intention, practice with care, reflect on what you notice, and review with gentleness. There is no requirement to do everything at once. The app is a companion for reflection, not a guarantee of change; data and analytics are prompts for inquiry, not judgments about a person.

I invite you to use these resources in the spirit of community. Keep what is helpful, share it with someone who may need it, and let us know where it can become clearer, kinder, more inclusive, or more accurate. Your questions, research, lived experience, and careful criticism can help make this a better and more truthful resource for people and communities everywhere.

Buddhi Align is a reflective practice tool, not medical treatment, therapy, or a replacement for teachers, mentors, or spiritual guidance.
