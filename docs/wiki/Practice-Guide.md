# Buddhi Align Practice Guide

I am [Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com/), and my invitation is simple: begin with one small, meaningful step. [Buddhi Align](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/) is here to accompany your practice, not to judge it. Let each step be an offering of care—to yourself and, through the way you move through the world, to others.

**Author:** [Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com/)

## Plan

Use the [Dharma Planner](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/dharma-planner) to name a purpose-aligned intention and one practical next action. Choose something honest and reachable: a commitment you can carry with steadiness and compassion.

## Practice

Choose the module that meets you where you are: [Karma Yoga](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/karma-yoga) for service, [Dhyana Meditation](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/dhyana-meditation) for meditation notes, [Bhakti Journal](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/bhakti-journal) for gratitude and devotion, or [Jnana Reflection](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/jnana-reflection) for inquiry and study. The purpose is not to perform a perfect practice, but to return—with curiosity and kindness—to what matters.

## Reflect and review

Observe recurring patterns in the [Vasana Tracker](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/vasana-tracker), then use [Motivation Analytics](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/motivation-analytics) for a calm view of practice consistency. Let what you see be information, not a verdict. For the official framework and FAQ, visit [About Buddhi Align](https://buddhi-align.foreverlotus.com/about).
