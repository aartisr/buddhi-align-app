How to begin a Buddhi Align practice

Choose one small practice area, make one entry, and let repetition—not intensity—create a sustainable routine.

OverviewOpen-web referenceOpen Buddhi Align

1. Choose a direction

Use the Dharma Planner to name a purpose-aligned goal and a next action. A useful first step is concrete, small, and connected to what you value.

2. Record the practice that fits today

Log a meditation in Dhyana Meditation, a service action in Karma Yoga, gratitude in the Bhakti Journal, or an insight in Jnana Reflection.

3. Notice patterns without judgment

The Vasana Tracker gives recurring tendencies and triggers a place to be observed clearly. It supports reflection; it is not medical treatment, therapy, or a substitute for professional or spiritual guidance.

4. Review gently

Visit Motivation Analytics to see activity and balance. The aim is a useful next step, not a score to chase. For the official module guide and frequently asked questions, see About Buddhi Align.
